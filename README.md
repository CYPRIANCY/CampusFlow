CampusFlow

Overview:

CampusFlow is a lightweight ticket management system for campus IT and support teams. It helps staff log, prioritize, and track issues reported by students and faculty — so nothing gets lost in a group chat or a shared inbox again.

The Problem:

Campus support requests often arrive through scattered channels (email, WhatsApp, walk-ins), making it hard to know what's urgent, who's handling it, and what's still open. CampusFlow gives everyone one place to record a request, see its priority at a glance, and follow it through to resolution.

Main Features:

Create tickets with a title, description, and affected-user count.

Automatic priority based on how many users are impacted.

Validation to reject blank titles and invalid user counts.

Persistent storage so tickets survive a restart.

Unique IDs generated safely, even after reloading saved tickets.

View and manage the full list of open and closed tickets.
